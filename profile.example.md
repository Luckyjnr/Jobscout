# Example

Backend engineer, five years, Go and Python. Based in Berlin,
working remotely.

## What I actually build

Distributed systems. Event pipelines, job queues, services that
have to stay up. The problems I've solved that most backend
engineers haven't: cutting p99 latency on a service doing 40k
requests a second, and migrating a live message queue without
dropping a message.

## Stack

Go, Python, Kafka, Postgres, Kubernetes, Terraform. AWS.

## What I want

A senior backend role at a company where infrastructure is the
product. Small enough that I'd own a system rather than a slice
of one.

## What's a bad fit

Frontend work. Java or .NET shops. Agencies. Roles where the
backend is CRUD with no hard problems in it.

Anything where the title says engineer but the work is support
or sales engineering.

## Things worth knowing

I need visa sponsorship for roles outside the EU.
